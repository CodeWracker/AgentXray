OBSERVATIONS: With the wider field of view bridging shaft to joint, the

angling toward the ankle mortise) are consistent with the fibula
overlapping the tibia in this lateral projection, which is an expected
normal finding in a true lateral ankle view. I do not see a separate,
distinct horizontal band crossing straight across the metaphysis in this
crop; the metaphyseal flare and trabecular texture transition into the
joint region gradually, without an abrupt transverse lucent line distinct
from this oblique density. The tibiotalar joint space at the bottom of the
crop appears continuous and reasonably uniform in width. No cortical
break, displacement, or discrete fracture line is apparent along the
visualized tibial shaft or metaphysis.

wider-context crop, better characterized as a longitudinal/oblique dense
line most consistent with fibular overlap (or possibly a nutrient canal
following the shaft axis) rather than a transverse lucent band. I do not
identify a convincing separate transverse lucency in this view. No
fracture, displacement, or joint-space abnormality is seen.

likely explains part of the discrepancy between my earlier read and
