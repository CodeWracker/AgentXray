immature patient. I do not see an obvious displaced fracture fragment,
gross bone destruction, or joint dislocation. The anterior/plantar soft

POSSIBLE ARTIFACTS: CLAHE (local adaptive contrast enhancement) can

than it truly is. Upscaling (3x) introduces interpolation, which can
slightly soften or blur true edges while appearing to add detail that is
not from original resolution. Faint vertical streak artifacts are visible
in the dark background, consistent with detector/scan or upscaling
artifact rather than anatomy — these do not appear to overlap the bones of
interest but confirm the image has some processing-related noise pattern.
