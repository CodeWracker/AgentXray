Proposed Additional Analyses:

Analysis A: Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization (CLAHE)
- Question: Can fracture margins and cortical breaks be more clearly defined?
- Why: It enhances local contrast without over-amplifying noise.
- Expected Result: Sharper definition of the fracture lines in the ulna and distal radius.
- Artifacts: Potential amplification of radiographic noise in darker areas.
- Comparison: Should show clearer cortical outlines compared to the original image.

Analysis B: Digital Windowing (Level/Width Adjustment)
- Question: Can detail in the overexposed hand and underexposed mid-shaft be recovered?
- Why: Adjusting the grayscale mapping can bring bone details into a visible range.
- Expected Result: Improved visibility of carpal bones and the mid-shaft cortical detail.
- Artifacts: May result in "washed out" appearances in areas of extreme density.
- Comparison: Should provide a more balanced view of all anatomical structures.

Analysis C: Laplacian or Sobel Edge Detection
- Question: Where exactly are the cortical discontinuities?
- Why: Highlighting intensity gradients can pinpoint fracture edges.
- Expected Result: Visual maps of the edges of the radius, ulna, and hand bones.
- Artifacts: Highly sensitive to noise; may create false edges.
- Comparison: Should highlight the discontinuity of the bone cortex at the fracture site.
