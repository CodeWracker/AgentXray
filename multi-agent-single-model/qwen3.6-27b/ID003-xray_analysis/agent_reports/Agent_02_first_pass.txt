PROPOSED ANALYSES

Analysis A: CLAHE (Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization)
- Question: Can we better define the fracture margins and cortical disruption in the mid-ulna?
- Why: It redistributes lightness to improve local contrast in regions that are too dark or too bright.
- Expected Reveal: Clearer definition of the fracture edges and potential fine fracture lines.
- Artifacts/Misleading Effects: May amplify noise in low-signal areas or create artificial edges.
- Comparison: Should be compared to the original to ensure no new "fracture-like" lines are created by noise amplification.

Analysis B: Edge Enhancement (Unsharp Masking)
- Question: Is there subtle cortical buckling or secondary fracture lines?
- Why: It emphasizes high-frequency components (edges).
- Expected Reveal: Sharper cortical outlines.
- Artifacts/Misleading Effects: Can create "halos" around dense bones, potentially masking subtle lucencies near the edge.
- Comparison: Should be used to confirm features seen in the original, not to create them.
