OBSERVATIONS: The image presents six panels. Top-left shows the ROI placement: a red box over

CRITICAL FLAW: The blue "Calcaneus Control ROI" is not positioned over the calcaneus at all.
It is placed in empty background space to the left of the foot. The top-right cropped panel
confirms this -- it shows no bone structure, only dark background. This means the "control"
values represent background noise statistics, not bone texture. The comparison is therefore
invalid as a basis for detecting cuboid abnormality.

Interpreting the cuboid values in isolation: an entropy of 5.177, local variance of 797, and
energy of 0.042 are consistent with normal trabecular bone texture. These values reflect the
expected heterogeneity of cancellous bone with intact trabeculae. A lytic lesion would be
expected to show a focal region of lower entropy/variance (more homogeneous, bone loss), and
a sclerotic lesion would show higher energy (more uniform, denser). The measured values are
within the expected range for normal trabecular bone.

RELEVANT FINDINGS: The cuboid texture statistics are consistent with normal trabecular bone.
The analysis does NOT support the first-pass finding of a cuboid abnormality. However, the
conclusion is limited by a critical flaw: the control ROI was placed in empty background
rather than on the calcaneus, rendering the comparative analysis invalid.

RELATION TO ORIGINAL IMAGE: The cuboid ROI crop (top-middle panel) shows a trabecular pattern
that appears structurally coherent -- no obvious focal defect, sclerotic focus, or
architectural collapse. This is consistent with the CLAHE-enhanced view (image 04) and
reinforces the conclusion that the first-pass finding was likely a projection artifact.

POSSIBLE ARTIFACTS: The primary artifact is the misplaced control ROI, which invalidates the
entire comparative framework. The analysis compares bone to background, which yields dramatic

the cuboid ROI may include portions of adjacent structures (calcaneus anterior process,
metatarsal bases) due to superimposition in the lateral projection, slightly contaminating
the texture measurement.

CONFIDENCE: Moderate for the conclusion (cuboid texture is normal). The conclusion would be
higher confidence if the control ROI had been correctly placed on the calcaneus to provide a
