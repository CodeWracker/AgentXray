MISLEADING-ARTIFACT RISK:
- Edge/high-pass filters (A01-P5, A02-P2, A03-P3, A04-P3, A06-P1): the highest false-positive
  cluster. Film grain, the faint vertical streaks, and scratches become "linear lucencies";
  halos/double-edges mimic periosteal reaction or a double cortex; overlapping intact
  cortices mimic a transverse fracture line. Guards (all agents converge): every candidate
  line must exist in the unprocessed original at the same location, be continuous, and be
  anatomically plausible (not crossing a joint space; ends meeting cortical margins). Add:
  run at >=2 filter strengths - a real line persists, noise lines migrate.
- A04-P7 (deconvolution): highest risk in the council. Phantom streaks are its signature
  artifact and look exactly like the fracture lines being hunted; strong
  regularization-strength dependence. Never a source of positive findings (see vote).
- A03-P3 median pre-denoising: may erase the very subtle line being sought; a no-denoise
  variant is required.
- CLAHE (cluster A): tile-boundary blockiness, noise speckles mimicking subchondral cysts,
  "waxy" smearing; over-equalization can fabricate a border around nothing. Best guard on
  offer: A02-P1's two-clip-limit bracketing plus original cross-check.
- A02-P3 bone subtraction: residual halo at bone margins misread as calcification/foreign
  body; prefer re-windowing or the contour approaches (A04-P4, A06-P3).
- Flat-field (A02-P5, A03-P1, A05-P4, A06-P5): over-correction creates curved brightness
  artifacts that mimic periosteal reaction (A06-P5 warns of this itself); can erase genuine
  broad low-contrast soft-tissue swelling; the collimation/field edge at the right border is
  a classic fracture-line mimic - identify it by straightness and continuation through
  non-bone (A05-P4, A06-P5).
- A06-P2 magnification: interpolation adds no resolution; Lanczos ringing mimics osteophytes;
  "mm" joint-space widths are UNSUPPORTED - there is no scale marker or radiopaque
  reference in the image, so report pixels only, qualitatively.
- A05-P2 denoising: strength-dependent; over-denoising can mask a real lesion or create
  blotches - the exact thing it must adjudicate. Run two conservative strengths; only
  structures persisting across both count.
- A06-P3 segmentation: thresholding breaks at the beam-field edge/scratches; the overlay
  must be verified point-by-point before measuring.
Universal guard (all six agents agree; make mandatory): the unprocessed original is the
arbiter. Enhancement/correction may only reveal, never create. A finding with no faint
counterpart at the same location in the original is discarded as an artifact.
