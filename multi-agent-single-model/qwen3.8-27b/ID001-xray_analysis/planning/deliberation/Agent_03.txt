enhancement (CLAHE/windowing) as the core tool, (b) edge/high-pass screening for
occult fracture and cortical integrity, (c) quantitative angular measurement,
(d) soft-tissue contour analysis, and (e) flat-field/de-vignetting correction.
Five distinct analyses are well justified and should all be executed, in this
priority order:
  1. True-lateral/projection check (A02-P4, A05-P5, A06-P4) - prerequisite: it
     determines whether ANY angular or joint-space interpretation on this single
     view is valid. Do this first.

     A05-P3) - adjudicates the spur disagreement (see PRESERVED DISAGREEMENTS).
  3. Talar body/neck denoise + local windowing (A05-P1, A05-P2) - the only

  4. Forefoot/right-edge visibility recovery (A03-P1, A05-P4, A06-P5, A04-P2) -
     de-vignetting of the dark right third plus explicit crop-boundary

  5. Angular measurement set (A01-P3, A02-P4, A03-P4, A04-P5, A05-P5, A06-P4) -
     six agents independently proposed this; redundancy here reflects genuine
     value: Böhler's angle addresses the unasked question of an old/healed
     calcaneal fracture, and pitch/position numbers document the plantarflexed

Single-agent proposals with real added value: A04-P6 (noise-floor/detection-limit

MISLEADING-ARTIFACT RISK:
- A04-P7 deconvolution: HIGHEST RISK in the whole set (self-acknowledged).
  Regularized deconvolution of a grainy rescan is notorious for phantom linear
  structures that look exactly like fracture lines. Gate: accept a line only if
  it persists across multiple regularization strengths AND is traceable in the
  unprocessed original. One setting only, no persistence across settings =
  reject.
- Bone attenuation subtraction (A02-P3): subtraction halos around bone margins
  can be misread as soft-tissue calcification or a foreign body. Treat any
  "density" adjacent to a bone edge on the subtracted image as suspect by
  default.
- CLAHE speckle (A01-P1, A02-P1, A05-P1): amplified film grain and tile-boundary
  blockiness can fabricate "subchondral cysts" and "sclerotic foci". The
  council's universal guard - every new finding must have a faint counterpart in
  the original - is mandatory, and A02-P1's two-clip-limit concordance check
  should be standard for all CLAHE work.
- Edge filters (A01-P5, A02-P2, A03-P3, A04-P3, A05-P3): halos on both sides of
  normal cortices can mimic a double cortex, periosteal reaction, or fracture;
  grain produces false linear edges. Guards: the overlay-on-original display,
  the "line must be traceable across its full length in the original" rule, and
  the orientation criterion (a real fracture cuts across trabecular flow and
  does not coincide with a cortical edge or joint space).
- Flat-field correction (A02-P5, A03-P1, A05-P4, A06-P5): the dangerous
  direction is NOT creating false positives but ERASING true low-frequency
  signal - a real broad soft-tissue swelling or a genuine density wedge has
  low spatial frequency and will be divided out with the vignette. Gate:
  compare corrected vs original region-by-region; if correction changes the
  apparent shape/density of any bone or soft-tissue contour, the corrected image
  is not trusted for that region. The collimation/field edge (A06-P5) is a
  classic straight-line fracture mimic and must be explicitly identified as
  non-anatomic.
- Magnification (A03-P5, A04-P2, A06-P2): interpolation adds no real resolution;
  Lanczos ringing (A06-P2) can fake an osteophyte or step-off. Use sharp
  resampling, and judge all final findings on the native-scale original.
- Denoising (A05-P2): risk is asymmetric and in the false-NEGATIVE direction -
  over-denoising can smooth a real faint lesion into homogeneity. "Mottling
  persists after denoising = likely true structure" is sound; "mottling
  disappears after denoising = grain" is NOT proven (a true low-contrast lesion
  could also smooth out). Two strengths should be run, as A05 proposes.
- A06-P2/A01-P1 pixel-based joint widths: no scale bar exists in this file, so
  "mm" conversions rest on an unvalidated local scale estimate; report widths
  in pixels as semi-quantitative only (see VOTES).

RESOLVES DISAGREEMENTS:
- Suspected bony spur (my first pass asserts a posteroinferior calcaneal spur;
  A01-P2 and A05-P3 treat any enthesophyte/Haglund change as a candidate to be
  confirmed, not a finding): the heel-ROI CLAHE (A03-P2) plus cortical edge
  delineation (A01-P2, A05-P3) is the adjudicating analysis. Decision rule: a
  true spur is a bony projection continuous with the cortex and matching bone
  attenuation in the ORIGINAL; a "beak" visible only after edge enhancement, or
  one whose contour is smoother on the raw film, is a normal variant or artifact.
- Talar mottled lucency (A05 alone flags a possible structural lesion; no other
  agent mentioned it): A05-P2 denoising with two strengths, followed by A05-P1
  local windowing, adjudicates grain/superimposition vs true lucency. If the
  pattern is reproducible across both denoise strengths and has a defined border
  in the original, escalate; otherwise downgrade to normal trabecular pattern +
  overlap - while noting it is not excluded on a single view.
- Positioning (A02 first pass: plantarflexed and possibly rotated off true
  lateral; A04 first pass: "approximately preserved"/neutral): the projection
  check (A06-P4 talar head superimposition, A02-P4 fibular separation) settles
  how far off true lateral the beam is; the angle suite then shows whether
  apparent angular deviations track with the quantified rotation (positional)
  or persist after accounting for it (potentially pathologic).
- Dorsal soft-tissue fullness (A01-Q1 uncertain; no other agent endorsed it):
  A01-P4's dorsal-vs-plantar internal contour control with intensity profiles
  separates a focal bulge (true) from a smooth global gradient (scan artifact).
- Old calcaneal fracture (nobody addressed it until A03-Q6/A06-Q4): A03-P4 and
  A06-P4 Böhler measurement converges these two; a normal Böhler plus intact
  posterior cortex (A04-P3) closes the question; a flattened Böhler reopens it.
