# First Look

**Modality:** X-ray
**Body Region:** Forearm/Elbow
**Projection:** Lateral view

**Structures of interest:**
- Radius and Ulna (shaft and distal/proximal ends)
- Humerus (distal part)
- Elbow joint space
- Soft tissues

**Potential abnormalities:**
- Possible fracture or alignment issue at the elbow.
- Evaluation of the distal radius/ulna for any cortical irregularities.

**Image quality/limitations:**
- The image is a lateral view, which can sometimes obscure certain fracture lines compared to AP views.
- Field of view is limited to the forearm and elbow.

**Open questions:**
- Is there a subtle fracture at the radial head or olecranon?
- Are the growth plates (if pediatric) appearing normal?
- Is the joint space congruent?
