This is a lateral radiograph of the ankle and foot.

**Modality:** X-ray
**Body Region:** Ankle and foot
**Projection:** Lateral

**Structures of interest:**
- Distal tibia and fibula
- Talus
- Calcaneus
- Midfoot and hindfoot bones
- Ankle joint space

**Potential abnormalities:**
- Possible fracture of the distal tibia or fibula.
- Alignment of the talocrural joint.
- Integrity of the calcaneus.

**Image quality:**
- Good contrast and exposure.
- The anatomy of interest is well-centered.

**Open questions:**
- Are there any subtle cortical disruptions in the distal tibia or fibula?
- Is the joint space preserved?
- Is there any evidence of a calcaneal fracture?
