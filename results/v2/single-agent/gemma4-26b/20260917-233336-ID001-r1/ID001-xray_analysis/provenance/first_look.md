# First Look

**Modality:** X-ray
**Body Region:** Ankle / Foot
**Projection:** Lateral view

**Structures of interest:**
- Distal tibia and fibula
- Talus
- Calcaneus
- Midfoot and forefoot bones
- Ankle joint space

**Potential abnormalities:**
- Possible fracture of the distal fibula or tibia.
- Alignment of the talocrural joint.
- Integrity of the calcaneus.

**Image quality limits:**
- The image is a standard lateral projection, which is good for assessing sagittal alignment but may hide certain fracture patterns visible only on AP/Mortise views.

**Open questions:**
- Is there a subtle fracture of the lateral malleolus or the posterior malleolus?
- Are the articular surfaces of the ankle joint well-preserved?
