=== 8. SUGGESTED ADDITIONAL ANALYSES ===

  [1] ADDITIONAL RADIOGRAPHIC VIEWS (AP and Oblique foot):
      Purpose: Disentangle overlapping structures in the lateral midfoot. Confirm whether the
      cuboid abnormality is real or a projection artifact. Assess the 5th metatarsal base for
      avulsion fracture. Evaluate the medial column and navicular.

  [2] IMAGE WINDOWING / CONTRAST ENHANCEMENT:
      Purpose: Apply bone-window contrast stretching to better delineate trabecular pattern
      changes in the cuboid region. May reveal subtle fracture lines or lytic margins not visible
      at current window settings.

  [3] LOCALIZED REGION-OF-INTEREST (ROI) ANALYSIS ON CUBOID:
      Purpose: Crop and magnify the cuboid/5th metatarsal base region with enhanced contrast to
      assess cortical integrity and trabecular architecture in detail.

  [4] CALCANEAL INCLINATION / BOOT HILL VIEW ANALYSIS:
      Purpose: Measure the Bohler angle and Gissane angle to rule out calcaneal fracture. Compute
      the talocalcaneal angle to assess hindfoot alignment.

  [5] CT SCAN (if not already obtained):
      Purpose: Definitive characterization of the cuboid abnormality — fracture pattern, lesion
      margins, cortical breach, sequestrum formation. Multiplanar reformatting would resolve
      superimposition completely.

  [6] MRI (if clinical suspicion for infection or tumor):
      Purpose: Assess for marrow edema, soft tissue extension, abscess, or neoplastic
      involvement. Would differentiate fracture from lytic lesion with high confidence.
