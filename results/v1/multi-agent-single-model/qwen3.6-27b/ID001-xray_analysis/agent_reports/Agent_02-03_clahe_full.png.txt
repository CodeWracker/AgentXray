2. The calcaneal spur is better defined and appears small to moderate in size at the plantar calcaneal insertion.

RELATION TO ORIGINAL IMAGE: The key findings (intact cortical margins, joint alignment, possible calcaneal spur, posterior sclerosis) are all supported by observations in the original image. The CLAHE processing has made these features more conspicuous but has not revealed any new finding that was completely absent from the original. The improved trabecular detail adds supportive evidence that no occult fracture is present in the visualized regions.

POSSIBLE ARTIFACTS:
1. Vertical banding in the background is a CLAHE artifact and does not correspond to anatomical structure.
2. The overall brightness increase in the background could create a false impression of soft tissue density changes; however, comparison with the original suggests the soft tissue contours are real.
3. Local contrast enhancement can exaggerate normal trabecular variation and may make normal areas of cortical thickening appear more prominent than they are.
4. Edge enhancement from CLAHE could make normal cortical margins appear sharper, potentially masking very subtle cortical irregularities that might be pathologic.

CONFIDENCE: Moderate to high for confirming the absence of major fracture or dislocation. Moderate for subtle findings such as early degenerative changes or very small stress reactions, as CLAHE can both reveal and obscure depending on the region.
