RELEVANT FINDINGS: Both-bone forearm fracture (radius and ulna) in the distal diaphyseal region. The ulnar component is displaced with angular deformity and possible mild comminution. The radial component is less displaced but clearly present at the same anatomic level, consistent with a traumatic both-bone injury pattern typical of direct impact or torsional force.
