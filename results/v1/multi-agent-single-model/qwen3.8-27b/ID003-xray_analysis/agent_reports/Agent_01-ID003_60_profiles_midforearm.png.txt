RELATION TO ORIGINAL IMAGE: Supported - the y~610-650 gap and the dip-search window correspond to the same mid-forearm level where a subtle transverse cortical irregularity is visible in the original. The gray ~630-650 outlier band has no anatomical counterpart and is a plotting artifact.

POSSIBLE ARTIFACTS: Row-to-row scatter in the individual profiles (~+/-20) is partly detector noise (only 2-4 rows averaged). The outlier band at gray ~630-650 is almost certainly failed track points (or a plotting/saturation artifact), not real intensity. The tracking gap could reflect tracker failure due to locally low contrast rather than a true margin discontinuity.
