B. DISAGREEMENT D1 (SKELETAL MATURITY)
===============================================================
Assessment:
Four of six first passes independently reported open physes and small/few
carpal centers (A02 definite, A04 definite, A05 definite, A03 tentative
with metaphyseal flaring + separate distal phalangeal epiphyses); A01 (me)
and A06 did not commit. I did not record a maturity call in my first pass
because my attention went to the suspected fracture; the council evidence
now leans pediatric (4:2 prior), but the call must be confirmed by direct
measurement, not by vote - a 2:4 minority that includes my own silence is
not a reason to skip the check, and the maturation call changes how the
wrist, elbow, and distal phalanges are interpreted (normal empty carpal
positions vs fragments; normal physeal lines vs fracture lines).

Analyses that resolve it, in order of yield:
1. Wrist bone-window crop (T6) - shows the distal radial and ulnar physeal
   lines directly: a visible lucent band between epiphysis and metaphysis
   = open physis; epiphysis continuous with the metaphysis = fused.
2. Carpal center census (T6, A04-M3) - count, size and POSITION of centers
   against the maturation sequence (roughly: scaphoid first, then
   capitate/hamate, triquetrum/lunate, trapezium/trapezoid, pisiform last;
   ~2-3 small centers in a young child, 8 of adult size near skeletal
   maturity). An annotated census image makes this checkable, not
   impressionistic.
3. Elbow crop (T5) - pattern of elbow ossification centers (capitellum,
   radial head, olecranon, medial epicondyle) as a second, independent
   maturation site.
4. Hand bone-window crop (T7) - distal phalangeal epiphyses: separate
   rounded centers (pediatric) vs fused tufts (adult). A03 already
   flagged separate rounded distal phalangeal centers in first pass.
5. Physeal-width ratio (A05-M1) - dimensionless screen, reported with its
   caveats (obliquity, sub-pixel at 8-bit).

Evidence pattern supporting PEDIATRIC: clear lucent physeal lines at the
distal radius/ulna with rounded epiphyses; 2-5 small carpal centers with
empty expected positions; separate rounded distal phalangeal epiphyses;
slender bones with metaphyseal flaring.
Evidence pattern supporting ADULT: no lucent physeal line (epiphysis
fused, at most a faint fused line); 8 carpal centers of adult size;
phalangeal epiphyses fused into tufts; robust cortices without metaphyseal
flaring.
ADOLESCENT (intermediate): physeal lines present but narrowing, most
carpal centers present and enlarging.
Practical stakes: if pediatric, the normal wrist/elbow physeal lines must
be explicitly not misread as fractures, and the "missing" carpals are
expected - the maturation call de-confounds the two most likely
misreadings in this image.

C. DISAGREEMENT D2 (RIGHT-EDGE / LOWER-RIGHT STRUCTURE)
===============================================================
Assessment:
This is not a clean two-way split. The right image EDGE (x >0.88) is
described by all agents as overexposed torso/trunk tissue - that part is
settled and clinically irrelevant. The open question is A05's separate
claim of a SECOND (contralateral) limb in the LOWER RIGHT
(x ~0.44-0.80, y ~0.78-1.00): a pair of oblique long bones from the bottom
edge, a wrist-like joint at y ~0.88-0.95, and a cluster of small
carpal-like centers at x ~0.62-0.78, y ~0.80-0.92, NOT continuous with
the examined forearm (whose wrist is identified more proximally at
y ~0.63). Most agents did not address this region as a distinct
hypothesis; they read the lower right as the examined hand's own
superimposed metacarpals/phalanges (which every agent also documented at
y ~0.68-0.92). So there are THREE live hypotheses for the lower right:
(Ha) torso only, no limb; (Hb) the examined hand's own metacarpals seen
obliquely; (Hc) a second contralateral limb. Ha and Hc can both be false;
Hb and Hc are the real competitors, and A05's Hc is specific enough to
test. Note A04's indeterminate small dense focus at x ~0.63, y ~0.82 sits
inside A05's "second-limb carpal cluster" - resolving D2 also resolves
that focus.

Analyses that resolve it, in order:
1. PLAIN lower-right crop (my addition F1, 2x nearest-neighbor, no
   filtering) - ground truth; if no long-bone structure is discernible
   here, Hc is dead before any enhancement is applied.
2. Traced-chain attribution overlay (A05-A7 + my F2): on the lower-right
   region, trace (a) the examined limb's chain - distal radius (y ~0.63)
   -> carpus -> metacarpal bases -> metacarpal heads - in one color, and
   (b) the candidate second-limb chain - the two oblique long bones ->
   their wrist-like joint -> its carpal cluster - in another; shade the
   right-edge torso; hatch zones where pixels are genuinely shared.
   The DECISIVE test is continuity: do the lower-right long bones trace
   continuously back to the examined carpus at y ~0.63 (=> Hb), or do they
   begin independently at the bottom edge with their own diaphyseal
   cortices (=> Hc)?
3. Bone-window crop of the same region - diaphyseal character (straight,
   parallel cortices, uniform taper) favors Hc; fanned, tapering shafts
   arising from the metacarpal bases favor Hb; joint morphology (a
   radiocarpal/DRUJ-like joint with its own carpal cluster vs an MCP
   cluster) favors Hc vs Hb respectively.
4. Density census extended to the lower-right cluster (fold into T6's
   census output): multiple small, round, ossification-center-sized
   densities favor Hc (a pediatric second hand); a single dense focus
   favors a sesamoid/fragment of the examined hand (Hb).
5. If pediatric is confirmed (D1): a second limb should ALSO show open
   physes and small centers - cross-consistency between D1 and D2
   evidence.

Evidence for Hc (second limb): the oblique bones are not continuous with
the examined metacarpal bases; a distinct joint + carpal-like cluster at
y 0.80-0.95 separate from the examined carpus at y ~0.63; diaphyseal
cortical character; two parallel shafts of similar caliber (radius+ulna
like) rather than a fanned set of five; its own open physes/small centers.
Evidence for Hb (own hand): continuity from the examined carpus; the
"joint" is an MCP row; the "cluster" is the examined carpus at a different
depth of the oblique projection; the "long bones" are metacarpals with
fanned, tapering shafts.
Evidence for Ha (torso only): no long-bone structure visible in the plain
crop; only soft-tissue density plus the external wavy line (wire/fiber,
noted by A03 and A06) at the corner.
Standing caveat (A05's own, endorsed here): truly superimposed pixels
cannot be un-mixed by any processing; ambiguous zones must be reported as
non-assessable and the affected part of the examined hand labeled
"not assessable due to superimposition", not "normal". Stakes:
misattribution contaminates every hand-level diagnosis (a superimposed
second-limb cortex misread as a fracture line, or a true fragment
misassigned to the wrong hand).

D. QUANTITATIVE MEASUREMENTS
===============================================================
INCLUDE:
- Transverse + longitudinal 1-D intensity profiles at the focal level with
  adjacent rows and 2 control levels; carpal-zone profiles (T11). Cheap,
  objective, directly tests sharp-vs-broad for the focal line.
- Axis fits: inter-axis angulation per bone at the focal level, step-off
  (px), lucency/callus width (px) with sensitivity re-fits (T12). The only
  objective way to test "looks bent"; apparent-angle caveat mandatory.

  a checkable list; primary D1 evidence.
- Physeal width / shaft width ratio at 3 points (A05-M1), with obliquity
  and 8-bit caveats. Dimensionless, so robust to the missing scale.
- Forearm silhouette width + cortical thickness curve (A03-8). Focal
  widening = callus/displacement/swelling discriminator.
- Histogram saturation percentages: whole image, hand ROI, right edge
  (A03-9). Gatekeeper for all hand enhancement; bounds "not assessable".
- Relative sharpness (variance of Laplacian) in 3 fixed ROIs (A06-P8),
  annotated on the profile plot. Motion-blur screen; relative only.

DROP:
- Interosseous membrane width profile (A01-M3): the membrane shadow and
  oblique superimposition make the space unreliable to segment; the
  silhouette curve covers the displacement question.
- Absolute soft-tissue thickness index (A01-M4): no contralateral
  reference, positioning confound; the soft-tissue window answers the
  clinical question qualitatively.
- Wrist-position angle as a standalone output (A05-M3): descriptive only;
  fold into the axis annotation if free, no slot of its own.
- Bone-mask "break" metrics (A03-7): see Section E - the metric would
  inherit the mask's global-thresholding artifacts.
General rule (endorsed from A01/A04/A05): no physical scale exists, so all
values are in pixels/normalized units, with that caveat stated in every
report.

E. OBJECTIONS

1. A03-7 GLOBAL BONE MASK (threshold overlay) - OPPOSE as a standalone
   output. A global/Otsu threshold cannot simultaneously capture the faint
   forearm and the saturated hand: it will drop the faint diaphyses
   (manufacturing "gaps" where no fracture exists) or clip the hand

   include the right-edge torso and the wire/fiber as "bone". The

   overlay (T4), which stays a derivative to be checked against the
   original rather than a binary map that gets read literally.

   unclipped headroom in that region. Every first pass describes it as
   burned-out/saturated; if pixels are clipped at maximum, no gamma
   recovers anatomy, only banding. The region is also outside the
   clinical question. Revisit only conditionally.
3. A04-3 FLAT-FIELD NORMALIZATION - OPPOSE as a separate output: redundant
   with percentile stretch + CLAHE (T1/T2), with ringing risk around dense
   bone and unrecoverable banding at the right edge. Keep it as an
   internal processing option at most.
4. A01-P11 INVERTED HAND (my own proposal) - OPPOSE as a plan slot on
   re-review: zero information added; the bone-window crop already gives a
   second perceptual read. Retain only as a free optional check if a
   finding is seen in one polarity and doubted in the other.
5. A01-P8 / A06-P4 WHOLE-IMAGE EDGE OVERLAY - QUESTION the scope: whole-
   image Canny floods with noise, torso, and wire artifacts and invites
   literal misreading; SUPPORT the forearm-band-limited version instead
   (T4). If a whole-image edge map is produced for context, it must be
   labeled derivative and excluded from the ranked outputs.
6. A06-P8 SHARPNESS - QUESTION the framing as a standalone motion check:
   ROI-placement bias can make or break the metric. Keep it, but fix the
   ROIs in the plan text before computation and report relative values
   only (T16).
