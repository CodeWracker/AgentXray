B. DISAGREEMENT D1 (SKELETAL MATURITY)
================================================================
Assessment. The first-pass evidence is lopsided toward PEDIATRIC, but the
split (4 of 6 committed, 2 silent) means we should not close this without
the two confirming analyses below. Pro-pediatric evidence, all from
independent inspection: open physes at the distal forearm (Agent_02, 04,
05; Agent_03 reports metaphyseal flaring and separate rounded phalangeal
epiphyses), small/immature carpal ossification centers with empty carpal
positions (Agent_02 "small/immature", Agent_04 "several gaps where carpals
are expected", Agent_05 "multiple small centers"), and slender overall
bone size. Crucially, NO agent reported the adult pattern: no fused
physes, no complete 9-carpus set, no full ossification-center complement.
Agent_01 and Agent_06's non-commitment reads as "not examined / not
visible," not as counter-evidence - and both of them independently noted
that the carpal/metacarpal zone is overexposed and partly clipped, which
is exactly where the maturity evidence would have to be read. So the
disagreement is best understood as "4 say pediatric, 2 could not tell
because of overexposure," not "4 vs 2."

What would resolve it (ranked):
1. Carpal center census on the enhanced wrist crop (T4, Agent_04 M3):
   an enumerated list with coordinates, compared to the expected sequence
   (scaphoid first, then capitate/hamate, triquetrum/lunate,
   trapezium/trapezoid, ...). A small count with empty positions is
   age-bracketing evidence; a complete fused set would be adult.
2. Wrist crop physeal review (T4): a finite-width lucent physis between a
   discrete rounded epiphysis and the metaphysis at the distal radius is
   the cleanest single open-physis read available on this view.
3. Elbow crop (T6): which ossification centers are present
   (capitellum, radial head, medial epicondyle, olecranon) brackets a
   different, earlier age window than the carpus and cross-checks it.
4. Physeal-width ratio (Agent_05 M1): a dimensionless physis/shaft-width
   ratio at 3 points on the distal radius - scale-free, robust to the
   missing physical scale, and a screen for pathological widening at the
   same time.

Evidence patterns to state in the final report:
- PEDIATRIC: discrete rounded epiphyseal centers separated from the
  metaphysis by a lucent physis of finite width; few carpal centers with
  empty positions; slender cortices.
- ADULT: no lucent physis (fused), full carpal set with fused intercarpal
  lines, complete elbow center complement.
If the carpal zone proves mostly clipped (T12), the honest outcome is
"pediatric pattern supported by elbow/distal-radius physes; carpal
ossification not fully assessable" - that is still a resolved disagreement
(4 pediatric / 2 not-assessable, 0 adult).

4. F2 (my addition): a transverse profile at y ~0.90 across the suspected
   second joint - a discrete lucent band between two bright ends supports
   a true joint; a homogeneous broad plateau supports a single blurred

Evidence that would count:

  cortex that terminate at a joint distinct from the identified examined

E. OBJECTIONS
================================================================
1. Agent_03 P7 (global Otsu bone mask + red overlay) - OPPOSE. A global
   threshold cannot simultaneously capture the faint forearm and the
   saturated hand; the superimposed radius/ulna will merge into one blob
   (exactly where a break would matter); and the right-edge torso band
   plus the lower-right wire artifact (Agent_03 2., x 0.85-0.95, y
   0.88-0.98) will be included in the mask and can be misread as bone.
   High artifact risk, low incremental information over the high-pass
   edge map (T3).
2. Agent_01 P8 (full-image Canny overlay) - OPPOSE as an output. Canny is
   threshold-sensitive, edges terminate at crop borders and bone ends
   (false "breaks"), and a full-image binary map invites literal reading
   of a derivative. The forearm-band high-pass (T3) gives the same
   cortical-continuity answer with less risk.
3. Agent_02 P8 (my own gamma right-edge) - OPPOSE, self-withdrawn. See T11:
   clipped pixels are unrecoverable and the region is outside the
   question; the slot goes to T10.
4. Agent_04 P3 (flat-field normalization) - QUESTION. Keep only if
   parameters are gentle; over-subtraction ringing around bright cortices
   can mimic periosteal reaction at the lesion. If produced, the focal
   line must persist on the plain crop to count.
5. Agent_01 P11 (inverted hand) - no slot. Zero artifact risk but near-zero
   information; the same crop already gets a bone-window variant.
6. Standing rule endorsed for all enhancement proposals (all agents
   stated some version of it, make it global): every processed output is
   judged against the plain reference crop and the original at native
   resolution; a feature visible only after enhancement is a processing
   artifact and must be reported as such. The plan must therefore include
   the plain nearest-neighbor reference crops BEFORE any enhanced output.
