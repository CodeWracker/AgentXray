Panel C (radial physis control; x=785/800/815, y=1095->1150): endpoints 105-187 with mid-band dips 93-108 - the same dip-with-dense-flanks pattern as panel A (though noisier), confirming the in-image reference morphology of an unfused physis in this same child.
RELEVANT FINDINGS: Q2 (olecranon): panel A shows a narrow, reproducible lucent dip with dense parallel flanks, morphologically identical in pattern to the normal distal radial physis control (panel C, dips 93-108 vs endpoints 105-187) - supports a NORMAL apophyseal physis over an avulsion cleft; no fracture pattern (irregularity, asymmetry, offset) is measured. Q1 (wrist): panel B does NOT show the dip-with-dense-flanks physis pattern; it shows a gradual asymmetric falloff toward the distal end with low distal endpoints - by the stated interpretation logic, a shape atypical of a crisp normal physis and leaning toward a lucent fracture line. However, no positive fracture sign is actually measured: there is no cortical step/offset in these curves, no wide irregular cleft, and the distal zone does contain a small centered rounded density (offset-dependent peaks 142/80/none) consistent with the expected small distal ulnar epiphyseal center; the low endpoints are at least partly geometric (profile length exceeds the epiphysis and ends in soft tissue). The profiles therefore do not adjudicate Q1 decisively: shape is atypical for a crisp physis, but no step or displaced dense fragment is demonstrated.

Nothing in the plots corresponds to a structure that is not present on the original.
POSSIBLE ARTIFACTS: No image-processing artifacts (native pixels). Residual confounders are measurement-geometric, not processing: (1) the 64-px panel B profile length likely extends beyond the small (~10-15 px) epiphyseal center into soft tissue, mechanically producing the low distal endpoints (39-91); (2) the 10-px offsets can miss the small epiphysis entirely (offset +10), exaggerating the "falloff" asymmetry; (3) native noise floor (±10-15 unit oscillations, e.g. the x=930 control) sets the reproducibility limit; (4) the panel C control is itself noisier than a textbook physis, so "crispness" of the physis pattern must be judged relatively.
CONFIDENCE: high for panels A and C (clean, reproducible dip-with-dense-flanks physis pattern); low-to-moderate for panel B (ambiguous shape with geometric confounds). Overall: moderate.
