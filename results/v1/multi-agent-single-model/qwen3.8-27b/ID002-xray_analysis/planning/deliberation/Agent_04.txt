MISLEADING OR RISKY PROPOSALS:
- A06-3's global histogram equalization: riskiest element in the set. Global HE amplifies background noise in the large dark areas and can manufacture phantom soft-tissue structures; overcorrection washes out bright cortices. Keep only the wrist crop and the ossification-center/age purpose; replace the global HE with local windowing.
- Edge-enhancement family (A01-3, A03-2, A04-2, A05-2): highest false-confidence risk overall. Edge detectors respond to normal trabecular borders, joint lines, superimposition edges, and noise; a "detected edge" is not a fracture. Acceptable only with the mandatory rule that every candidate line be confirmed by a density change in the unprocessed original (A04-2's intensity-profile test).
- Alignment overlays on an oblique view (A01-2, A03-4, A05-3): risk of false-positive subluxation from projection obliquity rather than pathology. A04-3's pre-quantification of rotation is the required precondition; A05-3's resampled "straightened" view adds interpolation artifacts, so measurements should be made on the original.
- A02-3 image inversion: minor orientation-confusion risk; low impact.
No proposal is invalid outright, but any positive finding that exists only in a processed render must be treated as an artifact — this rule appears in every proposal and must be enforced.
